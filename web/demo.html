LOT PASSPORT · demo

Every batch of goods gets a passport that holds its own money.

The buyer's payment sits inside the passport. An independent inspector stamps it at the factory. Pass: the supplier is paid. Fail: the money goes back. Afterwards, the passport proves the lot's quality to anyone.
